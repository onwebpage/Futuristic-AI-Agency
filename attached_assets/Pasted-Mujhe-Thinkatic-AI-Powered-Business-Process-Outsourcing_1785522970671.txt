=====================================================
DESIGN SYSTEM
=====================================================
Theme: Premium, modern, AI-driven corporate — enterprise SaaS product jaisa look.

Colors:
- Deep Navy: #081B3A
- Electric Blue: #2563EB
- White: #FFFFFF
- Gradient: Navy → Blue
- Cyan Accent (subtle use only)

Visual Style:
- Animated AI-inspired graphics (network nodes, subtle particle/gradient motion — 
  literal robot imagery nahi)
- Glassmorphism cards (frosted, translucent panels) for feature/stat blocks
- Clean modern sans-serif typography, clear type scale
- Generous white space

Navigation:
- BPO = primary top-level menu item with full mega menu (Healthcare BPO, Customer 
  Support, Sales & Lead Generation, Back Office, AI-Powered BPO)
- Technology & AI = secondary menu item, visually lighter weight

Interactive Elements:
- Animated statistics (count-up on scroll)
- Hover cards on service/industry tiles
- Process timelines (animated on scroll)
- Client testimonial carousel/slider
- Industry icon grid with hover states
- Prominent "Book a Consultation" CTAs throughout the site (not just hero/footer)

Performance & SEO:
- Mobile-first responsive
- Optimized Core Web Vitals (LCP, CLS, INP)
- Schema markup: Organization, Service, BreadcrumbList
- Har service ka apna dedicated indexable landing page ho (sirf anchor section nahi)
- Compressed images/videos, lazy-load below-the-fold media
